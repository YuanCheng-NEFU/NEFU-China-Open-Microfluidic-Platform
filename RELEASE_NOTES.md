Extract it and open `Software/PC_Control/TotalControl_V7_0/02_RUN_TOTAL_CONTROL_V7_0.cmd` after installing the dependencies in [Windows Setup](https://github.com/Astrid-Xu/NEFU-China-Open-Microfluidic-Platform/blob/v0.8.2/Software/PC_Control/TotalControl_V7_0/WINDOWS_RUNTIME.md).
